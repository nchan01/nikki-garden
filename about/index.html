A short introduction

I believe the best work happens between disciplines.

After more than a decade at Looking Glass, I still work with the team a few days each week across customer experience, technical writing and launches. The rest of my time is open for selected partnerships with other ambitious teams.

I like the messy middle: the moment when an exciting idea needs a plan, a team needs a better system, and somebody has to explain what makes the thing special.

That sensibility follows me everywhere, from Kickstarter launch rooms to building Notion workspaces to writing about notebooks and the way tools shape our thinking.
