01 / Before the campaign

Make people understand why it matters.

A strong crowdfunding launch begins before the campaign page goes live. I help teams clarify the promise, translate product complexity into a story people can repeat, and give an early audience reasons to care.

  • Product positioning and the core campaign narrative
  • Prelaunch content and email communications
  • Creator, early-adopter, and community engagement plans
  • Explainers, FAQs, and a content calendar that builds anticipation
02 / During the campaign

Turn interest into trust and momentum.

Live campaigns require a mix of good storytelling, operational discipline, and care for people who are putting their money behind an idea. I can lead the customer-facing side of that process.

  • Campaign page copy and launch-day messaging
  • Backer email, social, and product education content
  • Community moderation, questions, and feedback loops
  • Campaign updates and cross-functional communications
03 / After funding

Keep the relationship after the excitement.

Backers aren't just transactions. They are often a product’s earliest and most invested community. Clear post-campaign communication and thoughtful onboarding are part of the promise you made at launch.

  • Backer updates and launch-to-fulfillment communications
  • Support and knowledge-base systems for new customers
  • Onboarding journeys, tutorials, and product education
  • Community programs that outlive the campaign
