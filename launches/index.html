Working together

A launch partner for ambitious technology teams.

For founders and small teams bringing an unfamiliar product to market, I connect the launch story with a clear plan, useful content and thoughtful customer communication.

We agree on a project or milestone-based partnership, with responsibilities, deliverables, timing and a clear fee. The scope can span prelaunch planning, the campaign itself and the communications that follow.

Send me a few sentences about the product, your launch timing and where you need help. I’ll use our first conversation to understand the work and propose a scope.

Tell me about your launch ↗
01 / Before launch

Make people understand why it matters.

A strong product launch begins well before launch day. I help teams clarify the promise, translate product complexity into a story people can repeat and give an early audience reasons to care.

  • Product positioning and the core launch narrative
  • Prelaunch content and customer communications
  • Creator, early-adopter, and community engagement plans
  • Explainers, FAQs, and a content calendar that builds anticipation
02 / Launch and rollout

Turn interest into trust and momentum.

Bringing a new product to market takes clear storytelling, operational discipline and care for the people choosing to try something unfamiliar. I lead the customer-facing side of that work.

  • Launch copy and launch-day messaging
  • Email, social and product education content
  • Community moderation, questions, and feedback loops
  • Launch updates and cross-functional communications
03 / After launch

Keep the relationship after the excitement.

Early customers are often a product’s most invested community. Clear communication, thoughtful onboarding and responsive support are part of the promise made at launch.

  • Customer updates and launch-to-fulfillment communications
  • Support and knowledge-base systems for new customers
  • Onboarding journeys, tutorials, and product education
  • Community programs that grow with the product

Before we begin

A few launch questions.

When should we start working together?

Before the campaign goes live, while positioning, launch content and customer communications are taking shape. If you already have a launch date, tell me where planning stands so we can define what needs to happen next.

Do you work on product launches beyond Kickstarter?

Yes. I work with technology and hardware teams on product positioning, launch strategy, content, community and customer education, whether the launch involves a Kickstarter campaign or a direct product release.

Can you help after the campaign ends?

Yes. A launch partnership can include backer updates, launch-to-fulfillment communications, onboarding, tutorials and support knowledge bases. We agree on those responsibilities as part of the scope.

Can you work with a team outside Hong Kong?

Yes. I’m based in Hong Kong (UTC+8) and work remotely, with agreed overlap hours for teams in Asia, Europe and North America.
