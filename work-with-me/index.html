A few practical questions

Good to know.

Can you work with companies outside Hong Kong?

Yes. I work from Hong Kong time (UTC+8) and can arrange predictable overlap for teams in Asia, Europe, and North America.

Do you take on small engagements?

Yes, when the scope is clear and the project fits. I especially enjoy focused builds and fractional partnerships of one or two days per week.

Do you actually implement systems, or only advise?

Both. I can map workflows, develop content and knowledge structures, prototype and configure practical tools, and train teams. Complex engineering integrations or sensitive production deployments may need a technical specialist.

Do you publish a rate card?

I share a tailored fee and scope after an initial conversation so the engagement reflects the work rather than a generic hourly estimate.
