Featured experience / Crowdfunding

Building the story and the crowd.

Strong launches need more than a compelling product; they need trust, planning and follow-through.

My work spans the prelaunch narrative, campaign communications, community engagement, user education, and the practical follow-through that keeps backers informed after the campaign ends.
